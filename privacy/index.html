Sudoku Juroku: Brain Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Sudoku Juroku: Brain Puzzle (package/bundle ID: com.creacet.sujudoku) is a puzzle game developed by Creacet Software. You do not need to create an account or sign in to play.

Information We Collect

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Your game progress, saved games and records, daily challenge streak, hint count, and preferences (language, theme, music, sound effects, and haptics) are stored locally on your device only so the app can function.

The app requires no internet connection, plays offline, and sends no data off your device.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The app shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK. It includes no analytics, does not access your device's advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Paid App and Store Purchases

Sudoku Juroku: Brain Puzzle is a paid app purchased once from the store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Store payments are processed directly by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies, and the developer never receives your payment or billing details.

Notifications

If you grant permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device solely to remind you about the daily challenge. There are no remote servers and no push notifications. You can decline permission when prompted and turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Support Requests

If you contact us by email for support, we receive only the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your request.

Hosting of These Pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical data (such as your IP address) for security purposes under GitHub's own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Deleting Your Data

All of your game progress, saved games, daily streak, hint count, and preferences are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its local app data deletes all of your stored data.

Third Parties

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. If future versions introduce analytics, accounts, or cloud features, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at mustycetin@gmail.com.
