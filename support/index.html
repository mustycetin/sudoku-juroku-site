Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the game?

Sudoku Juroku: Brain Puzzle includes classic 9×9 Sudoku and 16×16 Juroku puzzles, Competitive and Zen modes, multiple difficulty levels, daily challenges with a streak, 4 themes, music, sound effects, haptics, and 16 languages. It is a paid app with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions, and it works offline.

Where is my saved game stored?

Your saved game, progress, daily streak, hint count, and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can resume your saved game directly inside the app without an internet connection or account.

How do I earn hints?

Hints are earned naturally as you play the game. There are no ads to watch and no in-app purchases to buy extra hints.

How do I turn off notifications?

The app only schedules local notifications on your device to remind you about the daily challenge if you grant permission. You can decline permission when asked, or turn notifications off at any time in your device's system settings.

Will my progress transfer if I change devices?

No. Because the app has no accounts or cloud servers and keeps all data locally on your device for privacy, your progress, saved games, daily streak, and hint count do not transfer to a new device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data also removes local progress.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sudoku Juroku: Brain Puzzle is a paid app with no in-app purchases. If you reinstall the app or switch to a new device, sign in with the same Google Play or Apple App Store account you used to buy the game and download it again from the store.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is handled.
